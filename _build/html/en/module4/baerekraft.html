Sustainability

Artificial intelligence has costs that go beyond what we pay to use it. Using AI services carries both environmental costs and social costs.

Many of these challenges are not unique to AI. Similar issues arise in the clothing industry, food production and other sectors. As individuals, we have limited control over how data centres are operated or how suppliers treat their employees. However, we have full control over how we use the technology in our day-to-day lives. That is where we can make a difference.

On the following pages, we will take a closer look at what you as a user can do to contribute to a more sustainable use of AI.
