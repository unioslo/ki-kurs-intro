Summary of chapter 1

In this chapter you have learned the most central concepts in the language of AI. Before you move on to chapter 2, you can test what you have learned about the concepts.

Test yourself

Discuss the questions with a colleague

  1. What is artificial intelligence, really?
  2. What is generative AI and how does it “learn”?
  3. What is the difference between an AI assistant and an AI agent?

Recap

  • Artificial intelligence is technology that can perform tasks we usually associate with human intelligence. For example understanding text, interpreting speech or analysing numbers.
  • Generative AI is a type of AI that creates new content, based on what it has learned previously.
  • AI assistant is an AI chat that has been given a predefined role or instructions, so that it can give more targeted and subject-specific answers than an ordinary AI chat.
  • AI agent is an advanced AI assistant that, in addition to giving answers, can perform actions in IT systems.
