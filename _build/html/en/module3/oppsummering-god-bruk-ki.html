Summary chapter 3

You now have a useful set of tools for using AI in a safer and better way. In this chapter we have learned how to assess and quality-assure answers from AI, and how you can use well-designed prompts to get better answers.

Test yourself

Discuss on your own or with a colleague

  1. What is a prompt and what should a prompt contain to ensure the best possible answer?
  2. What did you experience when you tried to improve your prompts?
  3. Which prompting techniques will be most useful for you?
  4. How will you assess risk when using AI in your own work tasks?
  5. How will you quality-assure AI-generated content?

Recap

  • Prompts are your way of communicating with AI. A prompt is the instruction or question you give to AI. The quality of your prompt directly affects the quality of the answer you get. To ensure the best possible answer you should: give context, specify format, define a role and give examples.
  • There are many different prompting techniques:

    1. Create a work plan for how the AI should solve the problem
    2. Ask the AI to explain why it gave the answer it did
    3. Improve the prompt step by step
    4. Start a new conversation if you notice that the answers are starting to drop in quality
  • Generative AI is not simply “reliable” or “unreliable”; it depends on what we use it for. A general rule is that AI is best suited to tasks where errors do not involve significant risk. If errors can have serious consequences, you must either be able to check and confirm the information before you use it further, or refrain from using AI for that task.
  • Artificial intelligence has several weaknesses and the content should always be quality-assured. To quality-assure the content you should:
    1. Assess the risk
    2. Write good prompts
    3. Check the answer
    4. Tidy up the conversation
