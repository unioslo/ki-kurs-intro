Welcome to the course: Introduction to artificial intelligence

Artificial intelligence is an advanced technology that is easy to use, but hard to use well. This is an introduction to what artificial intelligence is, and how you can use it in a good and responsible way. The course aims to give all employees basic training in artificial intelligence, so that they can safely explore how AI can be used in their own work tasks.

The course is developed for administrative staff, but the topics are fundamental and relevant to all employees who want to learn about how to use artificial intelligence.

Why is this course important for you?

Imagine that you could have a digital sparring partner who always has time and who can quickly analyse, summarise, translate, simplify or retrieve relevant information for you. Artificial intelligence can be this digital sparring partner.

It works fast and can process large amounts of data, but it has neither your professional expertise nor experience from UiO. Nor does it have emotions, ethical or moral judgement, professional discretion or awareness of IT security. That is why you should always use your own competence to assess which tasks are safe to use AI for, to evaluate AI responses, and to take ultimate responsibility for decisions. You must also remember that, as an employee of the state, you manage resources on behalf of society. You must therefore be conscious of your choices regarding new technology and adopt it in a way that preserves the trust between public administration and society.

We hope this course gives you the competence to use artificial intelligence in a good and responsible way in your job.

Completing the course

The course contains theory, reflection exercises, bonus content and practice exercises.

  • Theory: The course theory is meant to give a solid foundation for understanding the most important elements of AI technology, its strengths, weaknesses and how to use AI well.
  • Reflection exercises and practice: All the chapters contain encouragements to practise and reflect. Set aside time for these!
  • Bonus content: Throughout the course you will find opportunities to go a little deeper into topics.
  • Practical exercises: At the end of the course you will find practice exercises to train on AI and explore more about how you can use it in your own working day. These can be downloaded as a PDF.

The course can be taken all at once or split into two parts. If you want to take the course in two parts, we recommend taking chapters 1-3 as part 1 and chapters 4-6 as part 2.

The whole course takes about 60 minutes to complete, not counting the practice exercises in the final part.

Course chapters:

  • Chapter 1: Introduction to the “AI language” (8 min)
  • Chapter 2: Large language models (10 min)
  • Chapter 3: Prompts: Get good at AI (13 min)
  • Chapter 4: Responsible and sustainable use of AI (8 min)
  • Chapter 5: Approved AI services at UiO (14 min)
  • Chapter 6: Conclusion and exercise sets (6 min)

The estimated time is based on the number of words, and does not account for extra time spent on reflection and practice exercises.
